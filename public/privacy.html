Privacy Policy — MentorOS

Last updated: 5 July 2026

MentorOS is a personal learning-mentor app. This policy explains what data the app handles and why, in plain language.

What the app stores

MentorOS saves the information needed to run your learning plan:

  • Your learning goal, chosen duration, and study preferences.
  • Your first name, if you provide one (used only to personalise the mentor's messages).
  • Your progress: completed days, quiz results, a derived skill map, streaks, and XP.
  • Any study material you choose to upload, and questions you type into the app.

This is stored in a database (MongoDB Atlas) so your plan survives across devices and updates. The app does not ask for your contacts, location, photos, or precise identity, and it shows no ads.

Third parties your text is sent to

To generate lessons, quizzes, and answers, the text you enter and relevant parts of your plan are sent to Google's Gemini API over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. To suggest videos, topic search terms are sent to the YouTube Data API. These services process that text to return results; MentorOS does not sell or share your data with anyone for advertising.

Notifications

If you turn on study reminders, the app stores a browser push subscription so it can send you at most one gentle daily reminder. You can turn reminders off at any time from the app's Study reminders screen.

Deleting your data

You can permanently erase everything the app has stored about you at any time using the Start over button on the dashboard, which wipes your plan, progress, uploaded material, and stored questions. To request deletion another way, contact the address below.

Security

All traffic between the app and its server travels over HTTPS. Your data is stored on managed cloud infrastructure (Render and MongoDB Atlas).
